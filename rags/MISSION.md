# Mission: RAG and GraphRAG Expertise

## Why
You want to become senior-level at designing, building, evaluating, and explaining RAG and GraphRAG systems. Your current strengths in data engineering, Python, and architecture should become leverage: you should be able to turn messy enterprise data into reliable retrieval systems, choose the right retrieval architecture, and defend those choices technically.

## Success looks like
- Design baseline RAG, hybrid RAG, and GraphRAG architectures for real business datasets.
- Build production-grade RAG pipelines in Python with ingestion, chunking, indexing, retrieval, generation, evaluation, and observability.
- Explain when GraphRAG is worth its extra indexing cost, and when baseline or hybrid RAG is the better architecture.
- Diagnose retrieval failures using component-level and end-to-end evaluation rather than prompt guessing.
- Lead technical discussions about tradeoffs: data freshness, latency, cost, grounding, graph quality, chunking, reranking, and evaluation.

## Constraints
- Start from data engineering, Python, and general architecture knowledge.
- Teach through short, practical lessons with interactive checks and durable reference documents.
- Prefer high-trust primary sources, official documentation, and reproducible implementation patterns.

## Out of scope
- Deep learning theory beyond what is needed to design and evaluate RAG systems.
- Generic LLM prompting not connected to retrieval quality.
- Vendor-specific certification paths unless they directly support the mission.
