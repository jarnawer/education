# Mission: Databricks Semantic Model Domain

## Why
You want a focused crash course on the modern Databricks semantic and BI layer so you can confidently design and use Metric Views, Materialized Views, AI/BI dashboards, and related semantic-model assets in real analytics work. The goal is not to relearn Spark, orchestration, or general data engineering, but to master the layer that turns governed lakehouse data into reusable business metrics, fast dashboard datasets, and trusted self-service analytics.

## Success looks like
- You can choose between a Metric View, Materialized View, dashboard dataset, standard view, or table for a concrete reporting requirement.
- You can design a Metric View with fields, measures, joins, filters, and semantic metadata that supports dashboards and Genie.
- You can explain how refresh, caching, materialization, and query rewriting affect freshness, cost, and latency.
- You can build tutorials end-to-end in Databricks using the official examples, then adapt them to your own domain.

## Constraints
- Assume strong Databricks Data Engineering fundamentals: Spark, Delta tables, pipelines, jobs, and orchestration basics can be skipped.
- Prefer short, practical lessons with retrieval practice and directly usable SQL/YAML patterns.
- Ground lessons in current Databricks documentation instead of generic semantic-layer theory.

## Out of scope
- Introductory Spark, Delta Lake, Lakeflow, notebooks, clusters, and basic SQL warehouse concepts.
- Generic BI dashboard design unrelated to Databricks AI/BI dashboards.
- Vendor-neutral semantic-layer comparisons unless they clarify a Databricks design choice.
